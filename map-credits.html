Weather model data is provided by Open-Meteo and its national weather-service sources under CC BY 4.0. Snowline supplies custom colour palettes and a winter-weather interface. These displays are not an official Open-Meteo product.

Maps use Open-Meteo Weather Map Layer 0.2.2, licensed under GPL v2. Its source is available at the linked repository. The bundled runtime is unmodified.

Rendering uses MapLibre GL JS 6.10.0, with its licence. Basemap tiles are supplied by OpenFreeMap; OpenMapTiles and OpenStreetMap contributors are credited in the map attribution.

Terrain shading

Mapzen Terrain Tiles, hosted by the AWS Registry of Open Data, accessed 9 October 2026. Terrain is shown as shaded relief beneath the weather colours. Terrain data sources, required attribution and licence details.
